MAKE A USEFUL FIRST BRIEF

A little context goes a long way.

Share what you know. Leave room for the questions we can work through together.

01 /

Your project

Describe what you are making, who it is for, and what you want readers to take away. Mention whether you have an idea, outline, first draft, or finished manuscript.

02 /

Your priorities

Tell us where you most need help: the words, the design, or the next steps toward publication. Include an approximate word count and any required formats.

03 /

Your practical details

Add a target date, a budget range if you have one, and anything that is already fixed. If you have visual references or brand guidelines, mention them in your message.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you reach out.

Do I need a finished manuscript to make an enquiry?+

No. Describe your idea and its current stage. An early conversation can focus on structure and direction, while a completed manuscript makes it easier to discuss editing or design requirements.

Should I include my whole manuscript in the form?+

A short description is enough for the initial brief. Include the genre or topic, approximate length, and the kind of support you need. There is no manuscript upload on this form.

What if I am unsure which service to choose?+

Select “A little of everything / Not sure yet” and describe what you want to achieve. Your current materials and priorities will help determine the most useful starting point.

What happens when I save my project brief?+

The form downloads a text file containing the information you entered. It does not send an enquiry or create a booking. Keep the file so you can share it when a contact channel is available.

Can I include a deadline or budget?+

Yes. Include these in your project description. They help frame a realistic scope; saving a brief does not confirm availability, pricing, or a delivery date.
